Statutory Procedure

How SahakarSetu Operates

Three structured procedural steps designed to eliminate private platform surge pricing and guarantee worker social security.

Directives: MoC-SOP-V2.1
STAGE 01 manage_search

Step 1: Search & Select

Choose verified skill trades (electricians, plumbers, cooks, carpenters, caregivers) provided directly by registered local cooperative societies in your postal ward.

Available Trade Clusters:
Electricians Plumbers Domestic Cooks Carpenters Caregivers
STAGE 02 price_check

Step 2: Instant Booking with Fair Wages

Book directly without predatory middleman platform fees. All service charges conform to transparent, government-mandated statutory tariff cards updated seasonally.

Pricing Governance:

Zero commission overhead. Transparent hourly and task-based rates verified under Ministry schedules.

STAGE 03 verified

Step 3: Direct Cooperative Payout

Certified service fulfillment with verified Aadhaar/eShram linked workers. Payment is released from statutory escrow directly into the worker's bank account with guaranteed social security coverage.

Worker Security:

Direct Beneficiary Transfer (DBT) integration + Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima coverage per active booking.
